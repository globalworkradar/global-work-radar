## Trigger

Activate only when there is genuine human evidence from an exposed buyer.

Qualifying triggers:

- explicit Buyer Reaction
- question about use
- specific data request
- recurring-interest signal
- Paid Pilot interest
- commercial-terms request
- qualified reply or equivalent commercial conversation

Do not qualify QA, automation, bots, headless browsers, pageviews, raw clicks, or anonymous traffic as Buyer Reaction.

## Minimum Paid Pilot

**Name:** GWR 7-Day Custom Labor Signal Pilot

**Purpose:** answer one buyer-specific recruitment or labor-market decision with fresh GWR-observed evidence, without requiring a large contract first.

**Scope:** one buyer + one specific use case + one defined segment for seven calendar days.

Examples of an acceptable segment:

- Japanese-speaking talent demand
- Japan-eligible remote roles
- role/category demand relevant to the buyer
- selected geography or employer set when GWR has sufficient source coverage

**Deliverable:** one compact decision brief containing:

1. current signal and change direction
2. relevant observed jobs/opportunities supporting the signal
3. freshness and source scope
4. buyer-specific implication for hiring, sourcing, or client advisory
5. one recommended next action

**Boundary:** no claim beyond observed source coverage; no invented market-wide statistics; no custom dashboard/API/product build for the pilot.

**Commercial rule:** this is explicitly a paid test. Pricing is not published or invented before a real buyer requests the pilot. Scope is confirmed first, then commercial terms and payment route are agreed before delivery starts.

## Reaction → Commercial Route

`Buyer Reaction → Specific Use Case → confirm one segment → Paid Pilot terms → payment evidence → 7-day delivery → recurring-value check → recurring agreement/contract`

### Step 1 — Buyer intent

Identify exactly which of these is driving the reaction:

- hiring decision
- sourcing priority
- client advisory
- recurring market monitoring
- deeper custom data need

Do not respond with a generic thank-you loop.

### Step 2 — Specific Use Case

Reduce the request to one sentence:

> "Buyer wants GWR to help decide [decision] for [segment] over [time horizon]."

If this sentence cannot be written from the evidence, the reaction is not yet commercially qualified.

### Step 3 — Pilot fit

Offer the 7-Day Custom Labor Signal Pilot only when the use case can be answered from GWR-observed evidence without building new infrastructure.

If it requires a new dashboard, API, large research project, or unsupported source coverage, do not expand automatically. Record the gap and keep the request qualified but uncommitted.

### Step 4 — Terms before delivery

Before paid work starts, establish:

- exact segment
- seven-day start/end window
- deliverable format
- buyer acceptance point
- agreed price
- payment method / route

No contract, checkout, price, or payment evidence may be inferred from interest alone.

### Step 5 — Recurring-value test

After delivery, ask only whether the same signal would be useful on a recurring basis and what cadence is decision-relevant.

Recurring interest is evidence only. Revenue is recognized only after actual paid contract / commission / reward / paid bounty / payment evidence.

## Evidence ladder

`Payment Evidence > Paid Test > Contract / Agreement > Commercial Conversation > Qualified Reply > Buyer Reaction > Exposure`

Current buyer-facing site and CTA remain frozen while fresh Regular/non-QA human exposure is zero. This runbook changes commercial readiness, not the public experiment variable.
